About The Signal +

The Signal is an independent, manually curated news aggregator, unaffiliated with the publishers it links to. Every headline and link is selected by the editor. Follow a headline to read the original story. Headlines appear in the editor’s chosen order within each topic.

There are no automatic feeds or scheduled updates. The edition changes when the editor publishes new links. Headlines belong to their respective publishers. No tracking, advertising cookies, or account required. Your color theme is saved only in your browser.
